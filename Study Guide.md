## Conventions

- $\mathbb N=\{1,2,3,\dots\}$. Function composition is right to left.
- $\log z$ is the principal complex logarithm, $\operatorname{Log}z$ the set of all logarithms, $\arg z\in(-\pi,\pi]$.
- $f_{xy}=(f_x)_y=\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y\,\partial x}$ ($x$ first).
- "Proper" subgroup/factor excludes both the trivial and the whole object.
