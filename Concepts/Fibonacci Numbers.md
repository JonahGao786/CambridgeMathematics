# Fibonacci Numbers

The supplied definition is
$$F_1=F_2=1,\qquad F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}\quad(n\ge3).$$

Source: [[Numbers and Sets - Introductory Sheet 2026]], page 1, question 5. That question asks for addition identities, divisibility, the prime-index condition and last-digit periodicity. The student's arguments, including unresolved gaps, are preserved in [[Numbers and Sets 1 - Handwritten Attempts]], pages 6–7; those claims are not promoted here to proved theorems.
